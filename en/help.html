How Joot works · Deutsche Fassung

Joot helps you take more pictures and keep the best of them. There are three ways in: the Photo of the Day as a small nudge, challenges for one theme over a longer stretch, and albums for everything that matters to you. Your photos stay in your library – Joot only remembers which ones you picked.

Photo of the Day

Every day there is a small task, the same one for everyone. It is meant to work on a perfectly ordinary day – no trip, no special weather, just a few minutes. It doesn't have to be perfect: look around and capture what fits.

  • On the start screen, tap Photo from today and choose a picture you took today.
  • Forgot yesterday? In the Challenges tab under Photo of the Day you can still add the day before. Going back further is deliberately not possible.
  • If you like, Joot reminds you of the task – up to three times a day, at times you choose. Once the task is done, Joot stays quiet.

The Photo of the Day is always free.

Weekly goal and flame

You decide on how many days a week you want to do the Photo of the Day. The flame on the start screen counts weeks, not days – so a day off is no drama.

When you …then …
reach your weekly goal for the first timea flame lights up
make it again the next weekit becomes three flames
miss a weekthe flame doesn't go out right away: three become one, one becomes embers
reach your goal again from the embersit burns again

The number below the flame counts the weeks of your current streak. In the Search tab the overview shows how many of those weeks fell on each stage. You can change your weekly goal in the settings at any time.

Albums

An album is a place for pictures that belong together – a trip, a person, a project. Create one with the plus in the Albums tab and add photos from your library.

  • Note: Each album has room for whatever you want to remember.
  • Tags: In the album you set them for every picture at once. A single photo gets its own in full screen under Tag. Later, search finds your pictures through them.
  • Cover: Open a photo, tap ••• and choose Set as cover.
  • Places: Photos with a location show up on the album's map. If one is missing, you can set it yourself.

Deleting an album removes only the album, its note and its cover. The photos themselves stay in your library.

Challenges

A challenge is a task you collect pictures for over a few days or weeks – at your own pace. The plus in the Challenges tab gives you three ways:

  • Draw a random task – Joot suggests something from the catalogue.
  • Start a past one again – the same task, a new run. That way you see how you've grown.
  • Build your own – with your own category, name and number of pictures.

Once the challenge is full, you choose your favourite. It becomes the cover of the challenge and goes into your yearbook. You can also end a challenge early and resume it later.

Favorites and yearbook

The yearbook collects the pictures that meant the most to you over a year. You find it on the start screen under My year.

  • With the heart in full screen you mark a photo as a favorite. It then goes into your yearbook automatically.
  • With the book you put a photo in your yearbook without making it a favorite.
  • The favourites of your challenges end up there on their own.
  • At the top of the yearbook you can filter by Favorites or Challenges and switch the year. The plus lets you add pictures directly.

Search

Search looks through album titles, album texts, notes on photos and tags. Below it you find your overview: categories, your month, your most used tags and your streak.

Photo access and iCloud

  • Photo access: Joot needs access to your photos to show them. With limited access Joot only sees the pictures you shared; the others stay visible as previews. You can change this under Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Joot ▸ Photos.
  • iCloud: Albums, challenges and settings sync between your devices when they are signed in with the same Apple Account and iCloud is turned on for Joot.
  • Deleted originals: If you delete a picture in the Photos app, Joot can no longer show it.

Trial and unlocking

The Photo of the Day is always free. For albums and challenges, everything is free for 30 days. After that you unlock Joot once – no subscription, no ads.

If you decide against it, everything you created stays yours: you can keep viewing and editing it. Only new albums, new challenges and new photos in them need unlocking. If you already unlocked Joot on another device, tap Restore purchases in the settings.

Tips and settings

In a few places Joot shows you a short tip once, at most one a day. To see them again, tap Show tips again in the settings – they appear the next time you open Joot. That's also where you can see the welcome screens again, change your weekly goal and set up reminders.

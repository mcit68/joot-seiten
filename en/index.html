Joot

A photo book that asks

Photo journal for iPhone · Version 1.0, currently in beta

Joot gathers your photos into albums, lets you write alongside them, and gives you a small task each day: a subject, an angle, a reason to pick up the camera. Challenges let you work towards a series of images rather than a single one.

Your pictures stay where they are — in your photo library. Joot puts nothing on somebody else's server.

A beta tester? Then your invitation arrived by email. Feedback is welcome straight from TestFlight or at service@colihu.com.

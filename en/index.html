Joot

A thousand photos, turned into memories

Photo journal for iPhone · Version 1.0, currently in beta

Your library keeps growing, and the pictures you actually care about disappear among the rest. Joot lets you pull them out and build albums that deserve the name.

Then you add what a picture alone cannot tell: the place it was taken, a link to the restaurant that made the evening, a few lines about why you were there that day. A handful of loose shots becomes the memory of a moment, a project, a chapter of your life.

And if you want to get better

Set yourself a challenge. Joot gives you a small task each day, or you take on a series across several images — a reason to go outside, a way of looking you have not practised yet. After a few weeks you can see in your own pictures what has changed.

Your photos stay yours

They stay where they are: in your library. Joot copies nothing, uses no extra storage and puts nothing on somebody else's server.

A beta tester? Then your invitation arrived by email. Feedback is welcome straight from TestFlight or at service@colihu.com.

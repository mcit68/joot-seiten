Joot gives you a reason to head out every day – and a place for your best pictures and moments.

01

Photo of the Day

A small photo task every day: a line, backlight, something round. The flame counts how often you reach your own goal.

02

Challenges

One theme, several pictures: “Through something”, “The blue hour”, “Three layers”. A reason to head out on an ordinary Tuesday.

03

Albums

Trips, moments, projects: you choose from your library the pictures that matter most. Places, notes and links turn pictures into memories.

A thousand photos, turned into memories

Your library keeps growing, and the pictures you actually care about disappear among the rest. Joot lets you pull them out and build albums that deserve the name.

Then you add what a picture alone cannot tell: the place it was taken, a link to the restaurant that made the evening, a few lines about why you were there that day. A handful of loose shots becomes the memory of a moment, a project, a chapter of your life.

What Joot promises

  • Your photos stay with you.Joot copies nothing and stores nothing on someone else’s servers. Syncing only through your private iCloud.
  • No feed, no likes.You decide what was good – not an audience.
  • No subscription, no ads.Everything free for 30 days, then unlock once. The Photo of the Day stays free forever.
